Most recently, I worked at [OCLC](https://www.oclc.org/){:target="_blank"} in Munich for over six years, developing and maintaining software solutions used by libraries worldwide. Previously, I worked with companies including IBM and [Beijing Si-Tech](http://www.si-tech.com.cn){:target="_blank"}, gaining experience in software engineering, data warehousing, and enterprise systems.

I hold a Master's degree in Internet Technologies and Information Systems from [Leibniz University Hannover](https://www.uni-hannover.de/){="_blank"} in Germany and a Bachelor's degree in E-Commerce from [Beijing Jiaotong University](https://en.bjtu.edu.cn/){:target="_blank"} in China.

My main tech stack includes **Java**, **Spring Boot**, **React**, **TypeScript**, **JavaScript**, **PL/SQL**, and **PostgreSQL**. I have extensive experience in full-stack development, REST APIs, automated testing, CI/CD, and modernizing existing systems. I enjoy taking end-to-end ownership of software development, from understanding requirements and designing solutions to implementation, testing, deployment, and production support.

I value clean, maintainable code, thoughtful problem-solving, and close collaboration with product owners, designers, and international development teams. I'm also interested in AI-assisted development and exploring how modern technologies can improve software engineering.
