## Privacy Policy for zhuzhu Beruf B2 Lernen

__Effective Date:__ 2026-07-31

Thank you for using **zhuzhu Beruf B2 Lernen**, a learning app designed to help learners prepare for the German Beruf B2 examination. This app was developed by **Chenfeng Zhu**.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping the app simple, safe, and respectful of your personal data.

### 1. Information We Collect

**zhuzhu Beruf B2 Lernen does not collect, store, or share personal information** from users. The app is designed to work offline and does not require account creation, sign-in, or any submission of personal data.

We do not collect:

- names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses
- location data
- device identifiers or usage analytics
- photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or other sensitive data

### 2. Permissions

The app does **not request any special permissions**, such as access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

### 3. Third-Party Services

The app does **not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data-sharing services**.

If you download the app through the Google Play Store, Google’s privacy policies may apply to the store and your Google account. Please refer to [Google Play’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.

### 4. Children’s Privacy

We do **not collect personal information from any user**, including children under 13. The app is intended to be suitable for learners of all ages.

### 5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

### 6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at <a href="mailto:zhuchenf@gmail.com">zhuchenf@gmail.com</a>.
